# Instructor Guide

**Role:** *Instructor* (tied to your real person record, often via your
institution's login/integration).

As an instructor you use Tymora to see what and when you're teaching, tell the
schedulers when you're available and what you prefer, handle enrollment consent
where required, and book occasional rooms for course-related activities.

> New to the terminology? The [Glossary](glossary.md) explains classes, exams, and
> preferences. You'll find your items on the top-level menu and under **Help →
> Manuals → Instructor Scheduling**.

---

## What you can do

1. **Personal Schedule** — your teaching timetable: the classes you teach with
   their times, rooms, and dates. Also shows your **exams**.
2. **Instructor Survey** — tell the department your teaching **availability** and
   **preferences** (preferred times, rooms, distribution needs, course
   preferences, time you cannot teach). The Department Schedule Manager and the
   solver use this when building the timetable, so completing it well directly
   improves your schedule.
3. **Enrollments / Consent** — for courses that require instructor **consent**,
   review and approve/deny students requesting to enrol.
4. **Add Event** — book a room for a **course-related** or **special** event (a
   review session, make-up class, guest lecture). Requests go to the Event Manager
   for approval.
5. **Lookup Classes / Lookup Examinations** — the public search tools to check any
   class or exam time and location.

---

## Typical term flow

1. **Before timetabling:** complete your **Instructor Survey** promptly — this is
   your main chance to influence when/where you teach.
2. **After the timetable is committed:** check your **Personal Schedule** for
   accuracy; raise issues with your Department Schedule Manager.
3. **During registration:** handle **consent** requests for your courses if
   applicable.
4. **As needed:** use **Add Event** to book rooms for extra sessions.
5. **Before exams:** verify your **exam** assignments in your Personal Schedule.

---

## Tips

- **The survey matters.** Vague or empty availability gives the solver no guidance;
  specific, realistic preferences produce a schedule you'll like better.
- Prefer *Preferred/Discouraged* over *Required/Prohibited* in your survey unless a
  constraint is genuinely absolute — hard constraints can make you unschedulable.
- Everything is **session-scoped**; confirm the term shown before reading your
  schedule.
- Room bookings via **Add Event** are **requests** until the Event Manager
  approves them.
