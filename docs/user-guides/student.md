# Student Guide

**Role:** *Student* (tied to your real person record, usually via your
institution's login).

As a student you use Tymora to request the courses you want and get an actual,
conflict-free class schedule. The two tools you'll use are **Course Requests** and
the **Scheduling Assistant**.

> New to the terminology? The [Glossary](glossary.md) explains course requests and
> sectioning. Help for these tools is under **Help → Manuals → Student Course
> Requests** and **Student Scheduling Assistant**.

---

## The two steps

### 1. Course Requests — say what you want

Open **Course Requests** and build a ranked list:

- Add the courses you want, in **priority order**.
- Add **alternates** for each — a backup course to use if your first choice is
  full or conflicts.
- If your **advisor** entered recommendations, they appear here to start you off.
- Submit. This is your *intent*, not yet your schedule.

### 2. Scheduling Assistant — get your schedule

Open the **Scheduling Assistant** to turn requests into enrolled sections:

- The system (student **sectioning**) picks specific class sections that fit
  together without time conflicts, honoring your priorities and any reservations.
- Review the proposed schedule on the grid; swap alternates or adjust priorities
  if you don't like it.
- Confirm to **enrol**. Your **Personal Schedule** now shows your classes with
  times, rooms, and instructors.

Depending on your institution, sectioning is either **online** (real-time, as you
use the assistant) or **batch** (run centrally, then you view/adjust the result).

---

## Other things you can do

- **Personal Schedule** — your finalized classes and your **exams**, with times and
  locations. Printable/exportable.
- **Lookup Classes / Lookup Examinations** — public search to check any course's
  or exam's time and place before requesting.
- **Add Event** — where permitted, request a room for a student activity (goes to
  the Event Manager for approval).

---

## Tips

- **Rank honestly and add alternates.** Priorities and alternates are exactly how
  the assistant resolves clashes and full sections — a request with no alternate
  can leave a hole in your schedule.
- **Submit early.** Seats and preferred times go fast once registration opens.
- A **full course** isn't the end — a good alternate keeps your schedule complete.
- Check your **exam** times in your Personal Schedule once exam scheduling is
  published, so you don't discover clashes late.
- Everything is for a specific **term** — confirm the session shown is the one
  you're registering for.
