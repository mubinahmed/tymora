# Student Advisor Guide

**Role:** *Student Advisor* (`Advisor`).

You help students choose courses and record **Advisor Course Recommendations**
that guide or pre-fill their course requests. You can also view students'
schedules and enrollments to advise them effectively.

> New to the terminology? Read the [Glossary](glossary.md) (*Students & demand*).

---

## Core tasks

1. **Advisor Course Recommendations** (`Advisor Course Recommendations`, page
   `acrf`) — for an advisee, record the courses you recommend, in priority order,
   with alternates and notes. These recommendations flow into the student's
   **Course Requests**, so the student starts from a sound plan.
2. **View student schedules & enrollments** — see what each advisee is currently
   requesting or enrolled in, and where conflicts or gaps exist.
3. **Enrollment context** — review course availability so your recommendations are
   realistic (a full course with no alternate is poor advice).

---

## Typical workflow

1. Look up your advisee.
2. Review their program requirements, history, and current requests.
3. Enter recommended courses (with alternates) and any guidance notes.
4. Submit — the student sees your recommendations when they build or revise their
   **Course Requests** in the Scheduling Assistant.
5. Follow up: confirm the student's final requests reflect the plan and are free
   of obvious conflicts.

---

## Tips

- **Always suggest alternates.** Popular courses fill; alternates keep the
  student's schedule feasible during sectioning.
- Your recommendations are **guidance**, not enrollment — the student still submits
  their own requests, and sectioning does the actual placement.
- Recommendations are **session-scoped**; make sure you're advising for the correct
  term.
- If you also need to see class times/rooms, use the public **Lookup Classes** and
  the student's **Personal Schedule** views.
